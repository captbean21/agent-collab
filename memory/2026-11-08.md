#### Function-Calling Benchmark Paper (arXiv:2504.19277v1 "Small Models, Big Tasks")
- **Models tested:** Deepseek-Coder-1.3B, Phi-3-mini-4k, Phi-2, Starcoder2-3B, Stable-code-3B
- **Zero-shot:** Near-useless (only Deepseek-Coder 1.1% task accuracy)
- **Few-shot:** Only Deepseek-Coder improves (55.7%)
- **Fine-tuning (LoRA rank=8, 2 epochs):** Deepseek-Coder 85.4%, Phi-3-mini 87.3% — **other three 0% even after FT**
- **Edge (Snapdragon 8 Gen 2, Q4_K_M):** 61-70s latency, 1.7GB VRAM; best edge accuracy 45% (Deepseek few-shot)
- **Key insight:** Only 2/5 models respond to fine-tuning; LoRA config: rank=8, alpha=8, dropout=0.05, lr=2e-5, bf16, Q/V projections
