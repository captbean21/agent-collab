### Synthwave Production Techniques (Web Research)
- **Sidechain compression**: bass ducks on kick/snare — signature "pumping" effect, highest impact upgrade
- **Bass design**: saw/square mix, LPF 400-800Hz, filter envelope (A=0, D=200-400ms, S=30%, R=150ms), LFO→cutoff at 1/4 or 1/8
- **Drums**: 808 kick (decay 200-400ms, HPF 30Hz), **gated reverb on snare** (hall 2-4s + gate 300-500ms tail), hi-hats 8th/16th ±velocity, open hats on &2/&4
- **Arrangement**: 8-16 bar sections, gradual layering, 100-120 BPM (110 ideal), minor keys D-D-A-G + 7th/9th
- **Pads/leads**: Juno chorus (detuned saw), portamento 30-80ms, OB-Xd voice detune 15-25%
- **Reverb**: large hall, 2.5s decay, 25ms pre-delay, 30-40% wet
- **Organic touch**: VHS/tape degradation (wow/flutter, saturation), pitch drift
